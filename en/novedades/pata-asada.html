Roast pork leg, sliced to order with paprika

4 November 2026

Every time a plate of pata asada goes out, someone at the next table asks what it is. So, to save ourselves the explanation, here it is in writing.

What it is (and isn't)

It's a whole pork leg, roasted slowly until the meat stays juicy inside and the skin turns golden. It isn't jamón, it isn't tray cold cuts and it doesn't come in a packet. We slice it thin when you order it, never before, because pre-cut it dries out and loses the point.

How we serve it

Thin slices, a drizzle of olive oil and paprika on top. No sauces covering the meat: if the leg is good, it has nothing to hide. Order a full or a half portion.

Never tried it? Order a half portion next to the jamón. The comparison says more than any menu could.

Fancy trying it?Wednesday to Saturday, 18:00 to 22:30. Limited seating: booking recommended.
